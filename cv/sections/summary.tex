\cvsection{Research Summary}

\begin{cvparagraph}
  \textbf{I am a roboticist building truly independent robots.} My PhD research gives robots a sense of self-understanding, so they autonomously adapt their behavior to keep working when they, or the environment around them, change: systems that \underline{don't shut down when things go wrong}, but find safe, creative ways to finish the task without human rescue. I apply this where failure is costly and intervention is hard: long-term reliability for NASA space missions, safe contact-aware manipulation with the ROCC Lab, and intelligent package sorting at Plus One Robotics, where robots visually reason about failed actions, formulate recovery plans, and coordinate whole fleets of arms and conveyors to keep moving.
\end{cvparagraph}
